# Agent instructions: Tuturing

Tuturing is a hackathon project by Peyton Li and Xueming Xu: an adaptive AI tutor with two hierarchical reinforcement-learning policies (Curriculum Navigator, Teaching Strategist) and a separately supervised response model. The product requirements, system design, ownership split, repository layout, required commands, milestones and first tasks are in [docs/adaptive_ai_tutor_prd.md](docs/adaptive_ai_tutor_prd.md); README.md summarizes ownership.

## Working in this repository (everyone)

- Read README.md, brief.md and the PRD's sections 2 (ownership), 11 (layout and commands), 20 (workstreams and milestones) and 21 (immediate tasks) before starting. decisions.md records why things changed; append to it when a choice is made.
- Follow the PRD's ownership rule: change only files owned by your side (Person A or Person B) and change shared contracts only through the PRD's decision process. Build against the other owner's frozen fixtures and stubs, never against their in-progress code.
- Keep secrets, `data/private_raw/`, generated SFT examples and model artifacts out of Git, as the PRD prescribes; `.gitignore` already covers them.
- Speed matters: prefer the smallest change that moves a milestone, keep the CPU-only smoke workflow runnable, and commit in small steps with clear messages.
